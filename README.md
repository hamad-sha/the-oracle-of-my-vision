# The Oracle of My Vision

A personal research project exploring how effectively vision models can learn and align with subjective human aesthetic preferences beyond technical metrics.

**Status:** Early exploratory stage — established the virtual environment and directory for now, with a cosine similarity function tested.

---

## Origin Story

As an avid photographer, I have lots of friends and family asking me for my thoughts on the new photo they've captured, clearly expressing value for my take.

So, while waiting for the dentist in the patient chair one day, the idea of an LLM to tell someone exactly what I would find good in the photo (maybe with critique or suggestions) originated in my head, which after much thought and consideration, became a research project.

Instead of a full-blown LLM, I found it more robust to use vision transformers to see how far I can go with having an AI that can *"mimic my taste"* in photographs — so my loved ones can finally stop spamming me on my socials!

---

## Technical Stack & Architecture

* **Language:** Python 3.14
* **Deep Learning Framework:** PyTorch with CUDA acceleration (NVIDIA RTX 5080)
* **Vision Backbone:** Hugging Face Transformers (`google/vit-base-patch16-224`)
* **Embedding & Similarity:** 768-dimensional L₂-normalized feature extraction and cosine similarity scoring.
